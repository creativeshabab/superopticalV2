# Persona: Customer / Eyewear Buyer

- **Persona ID:** P-13
- **Role Category:** End-User & Optical Consumer
- **Target Organization:** Retail Optical Shopper / Patient

---

## 1. Responsibilities & Goals
- **Responsibilities:** Choosing comfortable and stylish frames, undergoing visual refraction examination, paying for purchases, collecting completed spectacles, adhering to optical care advice.
- **Goals:** Sharp, comfortable vision from newly prescribed glasses; transparent pricing with itemized frame and lens breakdown; fast and clear updates on when spectacles are ready for collection.

---

## 2. Typical Touchpoints & Interactions
- Undergoes eye refraction test in clinic.
- Tries on optical frames and sunglasses in showroom.
- Receives instant digital copy of optical prescription via WhatsApp or SMS.
- Receives automated order status notifications ("Your glasses have been edged and passed QC!").
- Collects finished spectacles at store counter, verifies comfort, and pays remaining balance.

---

## 3. Permissions & Access Scope
- **Permissions:** Customer self-service read-only access (via secure web link / QR code on receipt).
- **Scope:** Own profile, own family members, own prescriptions, own orders.

---

## 4. Key Workflows
1. **Order Pickup & Verification:** Receive WhatsApp notification $\rightarrow$ Visit store $\rightarrow$ Try on spectacles $\rightarrow$ Optician adjusts nose pads $\rightarrow$ Settle remaining balance $\rightarrow$ Receive digital tax invoice.
2. **Prescription Retrieval:** Open link from SMS $\rightarrow$ Authenticate with OTP $\rightarrow$ View and download digital optical prescription card.

---

## 5. Common Problems & Pain Points
- Misplaced paper prescription slips when needing new glasses.
- Uncertainty about whether spectacles are ready, resulting in unnecessary store visits.
- Hidden costs for lens coatings not disclosed during frame selection.

---

## 6. Required Information
- Clear itemized order invoice showing frame, lens, and tax breakdown.
- Estimated delivery date and real-time status tracker.
- Full digital optical prescription history with issue and expiry dates.
