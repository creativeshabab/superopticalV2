# Persona: Owner / Managing Director

- **Persona ID:** P-01
- **Role Category:** Executive & Business Strategy
- **Target Organization:** Optical Retail Chain / Independent Practice

---

## 1. Responsibilities & Goals
- **Responsibilities:** Overall business profitability, multi-store growth, supplier vendor contracts, inventory capital allocation, regulatory and tax compliance.
- **Goals:** View consolidated revenue and store performance across all branches in real-time; eliminate inventory shrinkage; identify top-performing frame and lens brands.

---

## 2. Daily Tasks & Operating Rhythm
- Inspects executive dashboard every morning (yesterday's sales, store rankings, cash collection vs bank deposits).
- Reviews low-stock warnings and approves bulk purchase orders from major frame and lens distributors.
- Audits monthly gross margin reports and discounts applied across stores.
- Evaluates expansion of new branch locations or procurement of new optical lab machinery.

---

## 3. Permissions & Access Scope
- **Permissions:** Full tenant-wide administrative and financial access (`tenant:all`).
- **Scope:** Tenant-wide across all stores and departments.

---

## 4. Key Workflows
1. **Executive Performance Review:** Executive Dashboard $\rightarrow$ Filter by Store / Date Range $\rightarrow$ Inspect Revenue, Gross Profit, and Outstanding Receivables.
2. **Supplier Negotiation:** Review Brand Sales $\rightarrow$ Compare Purchase Cost vs Selling Price $\rightarrow$ Negotiate Volume Rebates with Lens Labs.
3. **Loss Prevention Audit:** Inspect Inventory Discrepancy Reports $\rightarrow$ Trace Stock Adjustments by Store Manager.

---

## 5. Common Problems & Pain Points
- Disconnected branch stores delivering delayed or contradictory sales numbers.
- Unexplained inventory shrinkage on designer frames without an audit trail.
- Fear of tax penalties due to incorrect manual GST rate calculations.

---

## 6. Required Information & Reports
- Consolidated P&L and sales by store.
- Inventory valuation and shrinkage summaries.
- Customer outstanding balances and credit exposure.
- Optical lab job turnaround times.
